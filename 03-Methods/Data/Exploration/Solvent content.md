Density value P calculated from the crystal cell and contents, expressed as per cent solvent.
P = 1 - (1.23 N MMass) / V
N = the number of molecules in the unit cell 
MMass = the molecular mass of each molecule (gm/mole) 
V = the volume of the unit cell (A3) 
1.23 = a conversion factor evaluated as:

$$\frac{(0.74 \frac{cm^3}{g}) (10^{24} \frac{A^3}{cm^3})}{(6.02*10^{23}) \text{ molecules/mole}}$$

where 0.74 is an assumed value for the partial specific volume of the molecule
